    How VeriFit decides
    
  
    Models are ranked by how well they predict points they were not
     fitted on, not by how closely they hug the points they already saw.
     That is why a high R² can still lose: a flexible model can memorise
     noise instead of finding the pattern underneath it.
    
  
    The number beside each model is its cross-validated error, so
     smaller is better. Open a model to see its equation, how much of
     the AICc support it holds, and any warning about the shape of its
     misses.
    
  
    The tabs under the graph are there to help you doubt the winner:
     Residuals shows what the model missed,
     Predict shows how far apart the models drift once
     you leave the data, Sensitivity lets you push the
     parameters within their uncertainty, Influence
     removes each point in turn to see whether the answer depends on one
     of them, and R² vs CV shows where the flattering
     statistic and the honest one disagree.
